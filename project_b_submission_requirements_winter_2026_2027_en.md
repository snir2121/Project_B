# Project B — Submission Requirements

**Course:** Project B (00440169)  
**Semester:** Winter 5787 (2026–2027)  
**Document last updated:** October 27, 2026

## Background and Objectives

As part of the academic requirements for Project B, students must follow the course schedule and submit their project deliverables by the specified deadlines.

**Project B does not require participation in tutorials or enrichment sessions.**

The course requirements include project registration, a laboratory safety briefing, a midterm presentation, and a final presentation and project report, according to the dates listed below.

## Course Requirements

### Project Registration

Complete project registration by **November 11, 2026**.

### Laboratory Briefing

Each student must receive a briefing in the laboratory where they are carrying out the project **before beginning the project**.

The briefing includes:

- Introduction to the laboratory.
- Laboratory working procedures.
- Safety instructions tailored to the laboratory's activities.

Arrange the briefing directly with the laboratory engineer in charge.

### Presentations and Report

- **Midterm presentation:** By **January 20, 2027**.
- **Final presentation and project report:** By **May 23, 2027**.

Presentations take place in the project laboratory.

## Administrative Procedures

### Reserve Duty

Students on active military reserve duty are eligible for separate arrangements in accordance with Technion regulations. Contact the course coordinator at **jo@technion.ac.il**.
