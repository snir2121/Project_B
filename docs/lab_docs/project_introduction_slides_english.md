## Slide 7 — Project management

1. Your project supervisor is your **first source of help**.
2. A **weekly supervisor meeting is mandatory**:
   - Set a regular day and time for the meeting.
3. Schedule weekly working hours for the project.
4. The laboratory staff are your **second source of help**. **Use us!**

## Slide 8 — Laboratory rules

1. **Safety:** Follow safety training instructions and **do not work alone**.
2. **Entry:** Only students working on projects may enter; **do not leave the door open**.
3. **Conduct:** No food or drinks; **do not attempt to “fix” laboratory equipment**.

## Slide 9 — Laboratory access, lockers and computer systems

- **Laboratory access:** Have your student card encoded/registered to unlock the laboratory door.
- **Laboratory lockers:** Students who borrow equipment may receive a locker in the laboratory.
- **Computer login:** Use your Technion **@campus** email account.
- **Project storage:** Every group is allocated an additional disk partition; the folder is automatically mapped on all laboratory computers.
- On laboratory computers, the partition appears as drive **P:**.
- You can also connect to the partition from a computer outside the laboratory by mapping the following network path (substitute your group number):

  ```text
  \\ee-ftp-stud.ef.technion.ac.il\Diglab_Projects\022024\Prj_<group-number>
  ```

- Local disks are write-protected except for **C:\TEMP**, which is cleared periodically.
- Save your files on the server drive **P:**.
- **Application server:** `diglabapps.ef.technion.ac.il`; connect with **Remote Desktop** from the Technion network only.
